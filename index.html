Research Interests

My research interests are broadly in Natural Language Processing and Large Language Models. Below are the specific topics I am focused on currently.

AI Homogeneity and DiversityLarge language models are known to produce homogeneous outputs, which limits how well they serve diverse populations.

  • How the composition of training data influences model diversity
  • How to build more diverse models through better decoding and training

AI for Scientific ResearchEfficient agentic systems that support scientists by automatically proposing research ideas, conducting experiments, and verifying results.
